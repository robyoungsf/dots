Press kit

Dots is a cafe in Roscoe Village, Chicago, serving coffee and light fare. 7a–7p every day at 2000 Addison St.

Coffee by Metric, Four Letter Word, and Hexe. Baked goods by Publican Quality Breads. Art by Brian Lotti, Michael Kempen, and Nancy Kalebich.

For interviews, additional photos, or anything else you can email us at hello@dots-cafe.com.
